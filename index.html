7 Diagnostic Definitions & Psychometric Triggers

Methodology and item combinations used to evaluate each condition

1. Lack of Academic Interest: Low Academic Motivation score (< 2.80) or poor intrinsic curiosity & coursework value (Q001, Q002, Q004).
2. Collaboration Deficit: Deficit in Leadership & Teamwork, Communication, or Peer collaborative study (Q028, Q091, Q100).
3. Chronic Procrastination: High delay in assignment submission, lack of task prioritization, and poor study pacing (Q032, Q033, Q038).
4. Screen Fatigue & Digital Strain: High digital workload paired with poor physical ergonomics and absence of micro-breaks (Q065, Q068, Q069, Q226-Q240).
5. Chronic Skipping Risk: Irregular sleep-study rhythm, punctuality decay, and disengagement from live classroom sessions (Q044, Q013, Q021).
6. Mental Burnout & Emotional Overload: Concurrent deficits in Emotional Regulation, Exam Stress Tolerance, Physical Vitality, and Resilience (Q050, Q051, Q166).
7. Imminent Course Failure: Overall Mean < 2.60, classified in Bad/Poor success tiers, with High Dropout Risk.
