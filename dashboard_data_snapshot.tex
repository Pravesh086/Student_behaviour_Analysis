\section{Real-Time Dashboard Analytics and Empirical Data Snapshot}
\label{sec:dashboard_empirical_snapshot}

To validate the scalability and real-time decision support capabilities of the proposed architecture, an enterprise-grade analytics dashboard was deployed on the complete empirical cohort ($N = 2,525$ students). The system processes $17$ psychometric, behavioral, and digital domains across $255$ standardized Likert items, representing $643,875$ empirical data points alongside $13$ demographic dimensions.

\subsection{Multi-Target Categorical Classification Breakdown}
The dashboard monitors three synchronized predictive endpoints:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Academic Standing (5-Tier Scale):} Exactly balanced across the 5 performance tiers with $n = 505$ students ($20.0\%$) in each category: \textit{Excellent}, \textit{Good}, \textit{Average}, \textit{Poor}, and \textit{Bad}.
    \item \textbf{Longitudinal Dropout Hazard:} Identifies $1,180$ students ($46.73\%$) in High Risk, $669$ students ($26.50\%$) in Medium Risk, and $676$ students ($26.77\%$) in Low Risk.
    \item \textbf{Career Readiness Index:} Classifies $505$ students ($20.00\%$) as \textit{Highly Ready}, $500$ ($19.80\%$) as \textit{Ready}, $506$ ($20.04\%$) as \textit{Moderately Ready}, and $1,014$ ($40.16\%$) as \textit{Not Ready}.
\end{enumerate}
